# Summit Fellowship - Confirmed Fellows Cohort

## 🏔️ The Skardu Scale-Up Fellowship: Design for Scale
**Dates:** June 7 – June 14, 2026 *(Includes Day 0 Arrivals to Day 7 Departures)* 
**Co-Hosts:** Taleemabad & Mulago Foundation

### The Challenge & The Objective
Pakistan is home to dozens of promising social enterprises, but few achieve true national scale. Most remain trapped in "project-driven survival mode"—securing incremental grants and reaching thousands when their models possess the potential to reach millions. The barrier is rarely a lack of commitment or competence; it is a lack of a definitive scale strategy.

Over this 7-day intensive retreat, this curated cohort of founders will step away from daily operations to answer the defining strategic questions of scale: *Who is your ultimate doer (the entity that implements at scale)*, and *Who is your ultimate payer (who funds it)?*

### The Curriculum & Methodology
Guided by the Mulago Foundation’s rigorous **Scale Screen framework**, alongside 1-on-1 capacity clinics with leading Pakistani faculty, fellows will relentlessly refine their Big Idea, scrutinize their impact evidence, and reconstruct their models to ensure they are:
- **Good Enough:** Does the solution actually work, with credible evidence?
- **Big Enough:** Is the problem large enough to matter at scale?
- **Simple Enough:** Can others replicate it without the founder?
- **Cheap Enough:** Can the ultimate payer afford to reach everyone who needs it?
