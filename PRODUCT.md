# Summit Fellowship Website — Product Brief

## Users & Audience
- **Primary:** Current fellows (11 social entrepreneurs) attending the June 7-14, 2026 fellowship
- **Secondary:** Prospective fellows and supporters (future use)
- **Organizing Team:** Taleemabad and Mulago Foundation staff managing the program

## Brand & Tone
- **Tone:** Aspirational, empowering, rigorous, human-centered
- **Personality:** Professional yet approachable; celebrates impact and scale; clear and direct
- **Core Values:** Impact at scale, transformative learning, equity, entrepreneurship, collaboration

## Product Purpose
This is an **information hub and community platform** for the Summit Fellowship. It serves as:
1. Central reference point for fellowship schedule, logistics, participant info
2. Foundation for future scaling (additional cohorts, resource library, community features)
3. Representation of the fellowship's values and professionalism

## Strategic Principles
- **Scalability First:** Data structures and design support multiple cohorts without restructuring
- **Clarity Over Decoration:** Information architecture prioritizes discoverability; every design choice serves a purpose
- **Mobile-First:** Designed for fellows accessing info on phones (many travel to Skardu)
- **Accessibility:** WCAG AA compliance; keyboard navigation; semantic HTML
- **Performance:** Fast load times in potentially low-bandwidth environments

## Anti-References
- Corporate SaaS cream (Stripe-like minimalism)
- Dark, moody dashboards (not an observability tool)
- Overly decorative startup aesthetics (gradient text, glassmorphism)
- NGO/nonprofit clichés (hero metrics, identical cards, low quality photography)
- Overly formal/academic (this is vibrant, not stiff)

## Register
Product (app UI; design serves the information architecture and user goals, not brand identity)

---

## Core Pages
1. **Home** — Overview, key stats, quick navigation, arc of week
2. **About** — Fellowship philosophy, challenge, solution, curriculum, frameworks
3. **Fellows** — Directory of 11 participants with search/filter
4. **Faculty** — Mentors and organizing team
5. **Schedule** — Expandable 7-day itinerary with sessions
6. **Logistics** — Practical info: venue, travel, accommodation, packing, FAQ
7. **Resources** — Frameworks, documents, toolkits (foundation for future content)

---

## Visual Direction (from brainstorm)
- **Aesthetic:** Modern startup (Figma/Notion–inspired)
- **Color Strategy:** Committed (strong primary color + strategic accents)
- **Primary Color:** Blue (reflecting Skardu/mountain theme)
- **Accent:** Orange/warm tone (hope, growth, energy)
- **Typography:** Clean sans-serif (Inter, Poppins or similar)
- **Spacing:** Generous whitespace, varied rhythm
- **Elevation:** Subtle shadows, clean borders (no glassmorphism)

---

## Success Criteria
✓ All fellowship information easily accessible  
✓ Mobile-responsive and fast loading  
✓ Clean, professional design reflecting values  
✓ Scalable for future cohorts  
✓ Accessible (WCAG AA)  
✓ No generic AI-slop aesthetics
