# Summit Fellowship - Faculty & Organizing Team Brief

## 🏔️ The Ecosystem of Support
The Skardu Scale-Up Fellowship is guided by a curated group of founders, funders, and system-level operators. This document profiles the faculty bringing scale frameworks and patient capital, alongside the core organizing team orchestrating the retreat.

---

## Part 1: Core Faculty & Guest Mentors

### Kevin Starr 
*CEO, Mulago Foundation*
* **About the Organization:** The Mulago Foundation operates like a venture capital firm for impact, providing unrestricted funding and support to social entrepreneurs who have solutions to poverty and climate change that are designed for massive scale.
* **Relevance to the Fellowship:** As the architect of the "Scale Screen" and the "Doer & Payer" frameworks, Kevin brings decades of expertise in helping founders reconstruct their models to be Good Enough, Big Enough, Simple Enough, and Cheap Enough to achieve national impact.
* **Links:** 🔗 [LinkedIn Profile](https://www.linkedin.com/in/kevin-starr) | 🌐 [Mulago Foundation](https://www.mulagofoundation.org/)

### Dr. Ahson Rabbani 
*CEO, ChildLife Foundation*
* **About the Organization:** ChildLife Foundation is dedicated to saving children's lives by providing high-quality, free-of-cost emergency healthcare services, managing pediatric emergency rooms and telemedicine centers across Pakistan's public hospitals.
* **Relevance to the Fellowship:** Under his leadership, ChildLife has massively scaled its operations from a few hospitals to a nationwide footprint. He provides fellows with a real-world, localized blueprint for scaling life-saving operations and securing government partnerships.
* **Links:** 🔗 [LinkedIn Profile](https://www.linkedin.com/in/ahson-rabbani/) | 🌐 [ChildLife Foundation](https://childlifefoundation.org/)

### Dr. Sara Saeed Khurram 
*Co-Founder & CEO, Sehat Kahani*
* **About the Organization:** Sehat Kahani democratizes healthcare access through an all-female doctor provider network that delivers affordable telemedicine to low-income communities and clinics.
* **Relevance to the Fellowship:** As a Mulago Rainer Arnhold Fellow, Sara successfully applied the design-for-scale frameworks to Sehat Kahani, scaling it to millions of consultations. She bridges the theoretical frameworks with the practical realities of scaling a digital health enterprise in Pakistan.
* **Links:** 🔗 [LinkedIn Profile](https://www.linkedin.com/in/dr-sara-saeed-khurram/) | 🌐 [Sehat Kahani](https://sehatkahani.com/)

### Sarah Farooq 
*Director, Acumen Fund*
* **About the Organization:** Acumen is a global nonprofit changing the way the world tackles poverty by investing patient capital in social enterprises, and developing leaders who can build a more inclusive world.
* **Relevance to the Fellowship:** With her experience at Acumen investing patient capital and her previous operational background as COO at Taleemabad, Sarah provides deep insights into how funders evaluate investability, scale strategy, and operational readiness.
* **Links:** 🔗 [LinkedIn Profile](https://www.linkedin.com/in/sarah-farooq/) | 🌐 [Acumen](https://acumen.org/)

### Nadir Shams 
*Founder, Friends of Pakistan Society & Fund*
* **About the Organization:** The Friends of Pakistan Society and Fund catalyzes transformational social change and supports social entrepreneurs driving systems-level improvements across the country.
