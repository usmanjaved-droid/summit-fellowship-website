What to expect.

02 · Weather

June weather
in Skardu.

Dry, sunny days. Cool mornings and evenings. Occasional thunderstorms in the late afternoon — they pass quickly. The sun is intense at altitude; a hat and SPF are non-negotiable.

Pack layers. The temperature swing between 6 AM and 2 PM can easily be 15°C. A light fleece or windbreaker covers most evenings.

The packing list.

03 · Kit

Documents & essentials

  • Passport / CNIC + flight tickets
  • A printed copy of your itinerary
  • Insurance card / emergency contacts
  • PKR + a backup payment method
  • Phone charger + universal adapter
  • Power bank (signal is patchy at altitude)
  • Physical notebook + a pen you like

Clothing & layers

  • Light fleece or windbreaker
  • Comfortable walking shoes (closed-toe)
  • Modest layers for cultural events
  • One smart-casual outfit (Demo Day + closing dinner)
  • Light sweater for evenings
  • Sun hat or cap
  • Sandals for the resort

Health & altitude

  • SPF 50+ sunscreen
  • Sunglasses with UV protection
  • Reusable water bottle (1L+)
  • Personal medications (named, in original packaging)
  • Lip balm
  • Diamox if prone to altitude sickness (consult your doctor)
  • Hand sanitizer + basic first aid

Frequently asked.

04 · FAQ
Do I need a visa to enter Pakistan?+
Most international fellows and faculty are eligible for an e-visa. Please apply at least 4 weeks in advance via the Government of Pakistan's online portal. The fellowship team will provide a letter of invitation on request — email logistics@summitfellowship.pk.
What if my Skardu flight is cancelled?+
It happens. We strongly recommend arriving in Islamabad at least 24 hours before your scheduled Skardu departure. The fellowship covers ground-route transfers in the event of cancellations — coordinate with the team immediately and we'll get you on the road or onto the next flight.
Is altitude sickness a concern?+
Skardu sits at ~2,228 m. Most people acclimatize within a day. Drink lots of water, take it easy on Day 0, and avoid alcohol the first night. If you have a history of altitude issues, consult your doctor about Diamox before travel.
Is wifi available at the venue?+
Yes, but it's patchy. Khoj has wifi in common areas; mobile signal is intermittent across the valley. Plan for offline time during sessions. This is a feature, not a bug.
What about dietary restrictions?+
Khoj is well-equipped for vegetarian, halal, gluten-free, and most allergen-conscious diets. Please flag yours during onboarding and we'll handle it. Local Balti cuisine is excellent and largely accommodating.
Can I bring a plus-one?+
The fellowship itself is for fellows only — protected space for the cohort. We can occasionally accommodate a partner at the venue at additional cost; please reach out before booking flights.
Are costs covered?+
For confirmed cohort members, the fellowship covers all programming, accommodation, meals at Khoj, and ground transfers within Skardu. Fellows arrange and cover their own travel to Islamabad / Skardu — partial travel stipends are available on a needs basis.
Can I extend my stay?+
Absolutely. Many fellows extend by a few days to explore the region — the Deosai Plateau, Shangrila Lake, or even a short trek toward K2 base camp. The team can recommend local operators. Note that Khoj's reserved room block ends Day 7.

Anything else? Just ask.

Logistics questions, dietary needs, visa letters — the team is responsive and on it.
