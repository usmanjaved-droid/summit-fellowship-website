03 · Pre-reads

Reading before you arrive.

Three short reads we ask everyone to do before Day 0. None are long. All are foundational to the conversations on Day 1.

Essay · 15 min

Eight Words You Must Get Right

Kevin Starr · Stanford Social Innovation Review

The single best primer on the Mulago Mission framework. If you read one thing before arriving, read this. It frames Day 1 entirely.

Read on SSIR →
Essay · 10 min

The Trouble with Impact Investing

Kevin Starr · SSIR

Frames the funder/operator conversation that runs all week. Useful context for Day 4's parallel funder track.

Read on SSIR →
Article · 12 min

Doer and Payer at Scale

Mulago Foundation · Working Paper

The framework everything else hangs on. Read this twice. The second time, with your own model in front of you.

Read PDF →
Optional · 20 min

Scaling: The Surprising Mathematics of Life and Death

Geoffrey West · TED Talk

A wild card. Why scale obeys mathematical laws across biology, cities, and corporations — and what it means for designing organizations.
