# 🏔️ Summit Fellowship 2026: Master Program Brief & Itinerary (Updated)

## Part 1: High-Level Brief

**Dates:** June 7 – June 14, 2026

**Location:** Khoj Resort, Skardu, Pakistan

**Cohort:** 11 promising social enterprises (Pakistan-based)

**Co-hosts:** Taleemabad & Mulago Foundation

### The Core Philosophy
> *Transform organizations from project-driven survival mode to scale-ready impact machines through intensive design work, peer learning, and strategic preparation for funding conversations.*

### The Arc of the Week
- **Day 1:** Mulago Basics *(Mission, Big Idea, Theory, Model, Behaviors, Doer & Payer at Scale)*
- **Day 2:** Scale Strategy + Impact Evidence *(Scale Screen framework)*
- **Day 3:** 1-on-1 Clinics *(Outdoor capacity sessions with Pakistani faculty)*
- **Day 4:** Iterative Organization *(Theory, methods, data flows, and culture)*
- **Day 5:** Communications + Capacity Clinics *(Clear narrative and 10-min pitching)*
- **Day 6:** Demo Day + Celebrations *(Participant demos followed by closing dinner)*
- **Day 7:** Departures *(Optional Karakoram excursion)*
